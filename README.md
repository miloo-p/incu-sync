## 📜 About The Project

**OpenEar Alchemist** is an unofficial configuration companion for **Equalizer APO**. It serves as a digital transmutation tool, turning the "lead" of hearing loss into the "gold" of clear audio.

Configuring system-wide audio equalization based on medical data is often a complex, manual ritual. This tool aims to automate the process, bridging the gap between a medical **Audiogram** and a ready-to-use **Equalizer APO config file**.

### 🎯 The Mission
As a hearing-impaired gamer and media designer, I experienced a magical moment when I finally calibrated my PC audio correctly: The "fog" lifted, and I could hear details I hadn't heard in years. It was a tear-jerking experience—a sip of pure clarity.

**OpenEar Alchemist** exists to bring this "Golden Brew" to everyone, removing the technical barriers so you can taste your world again.

---

## ✨ Features (Planned)

* **🧪 The Laboratory (UI):** A visual, interactive interface to input your hearing thresholds (dB HL).
* **🔥 The Transmutation (Logic):** Automatic calculation of the necessary gain values to compensate for hearing loss (based on standard fitting formulas like Half-Gain).
* **⚱️ The Elixir (Export):** One-click generation of a `config.txt` file ready for Equalizer APO.
* **👁️ Future Vision:** OCR integration to scan paper audiograms directly via camera.
